## 5. What the ground-truth filter removed

A step exists only for an actionable element whose UIA name the harvester could confirm as printed (Tesseract on the element's box), and whose caption is unique on the screen. ## 7. Near-miss negatives

The benchmark's absent targets never share a word sequence with a name on their screen: `generate._absent_here` treats "Save" as *not absent* from a screen that shows "Save As", and leaves such captions out. This section builds exactly those, by two rules that do not look at any scorer (see `near_miss_negatives`), and asks each locator whether the caption is there. A pass is a false pass.

| kind | negatives | `text=` | `text= exact only` | `desc=` | `desc= exact name` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sibling | 168 | 39 (0.23) | 1 (0.01) | 45 (0.27) | 0 (0.00) |
| truncation | 184 | 154 (0.84) | 3 (0.02) | 131 (0.71) | 5 (0.03) |
| **all** | 352 | 193 | 4 | 176 | 5 |

`text=` falls back from an exact match to a substring match, so it passes an absent caption that is the beginning of a present one; `desc=` passes one whose words all occur in a present caption. The two `exact` columns are a stricter rule evaluated here only, not shipped: the matched element must read exactly the caption's words.

The `sibling` kind is the fair measure: each is a caption that exists as a control on another screen of the same application and is not a control here. The `truncation` kind is a stress test of partial matching and overstates the problem: a tester who writes the first words of a caption often means the whole control, and OCR sometimes prints the shorter string as a fragment of its own (the exact-only columns show how often).

Examples (absent caption → element that passed it):

- sibling, `desc=`: `Extract files...` → `Files`; `Test` → `Test archive`; `www.7-zip.org` → `Folders`; `System temp folder` → `Folders`
- sibling, `text=`: `7-Zip` → `7-Zip`; `Add` → `Add to archive`; `Extract` → `) Extractfiles...`; `Test` → `Test archive`
- sibling, `text= exact only`: `7-Zip` → `7-Zip`
- truncation, `desc=`: `Cascaded context` → `Cascaded context menu`; `Icons in context` → `Icons in context menu`; `Eliminate duplication of root` → `Eliminate duplication ofroot f`; `Compress to <Archive>.zip and` → `a Compress to <Archive> zip an`
